## What the numbers mean

A typical bill separates the energy you used (measured in kilowatt-hours or therms) from fixed service charges and, sometimes, time-of-use rates that change by hour. Comparing months across a year shows how much heating and cooling drive your costs.

Knowing your rate structure matters: if your utility charges more at peak hours, shifting laundry, dishwashing, or EV charging to off-peak times lowers the bill with no equipment at all.
