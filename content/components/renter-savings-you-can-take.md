## Upgrades that move with you

You don't need to own to spend less on energy. Swap bulbs for LEDs, add removable weatherstripping and door draft stoppers, use smart plugs to kill standby loads, and — with landlord approval — install a smart thermostat you can take when you leave.

For bigger fixes like drafty windows or thin insulation, a short, friendly note to your landlord framed around lower turnover and fewer complaints sometimes gets results.
