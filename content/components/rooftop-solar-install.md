## From interest to installed

A solar project starts with a roof and shading assessment, then quotes from a few installers that spell out system size, production estimates, warranties, and price. Compare on total cost after incentives and the quality of equipment, not just the sticker.

Line up the federal residential clean energy credit and any state or utility programs before signing, and decide between cash, loan, and lease — each changes who owns the panels and who claims the incentives.
