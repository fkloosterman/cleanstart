## Follow the biggest sources

For a typical home, the largest carbon sources are the fuel burned on-site for space heating and hot water, plus the emissions tied to the electricity you draw from the grid. Cars, if you have them, often rival the house itself.

That ordering is why electrifying heat, water heating, and driving — while the grid itself gets cleaner — does far more than small plug-load tweaks. Start where the fuel and miles are.
