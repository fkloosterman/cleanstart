## The quiet second-biggest bill

Heating water typically ranks just behind space conditioning in a home's energy use. When it's time to replace, the choice matters: a heat-pump water heater uses far less energy than a standard electric or gas tank by moving heat rather than making it, much like a heat pump for your home.

Tankless units heat on demand and save space; conventional tanks cost the least up front. Matching type to your household's hot-water pattern is the key decision.
