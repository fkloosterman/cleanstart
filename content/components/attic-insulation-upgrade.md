## Cap the biggest heat leak

Warm air rises and escapes through gaps in the attic floor; in summer, attic heat pours down into living space. Sealing those penetrations and bringing insulation up to the recommended depth steadies temperatures and cuts heating and cooling costs.

It's often bundled with an energy assessment and can qualify for the 25C credit. Do the air sealing first — insulation over unsealed leaks underperforms.
