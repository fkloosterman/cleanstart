## Honest limits of rooftop solar

Solar pays back best on a sound, sunny roof owned by someone staying put. Heavy shade, a roof near the end of its life (you don't want to remove panels to reroof), very low electricity use, or plans to move soon can all stretch the payback beyond what makes sense.

In those cases, community solar or efficiency upgrades often deliver more savings per dollar. It's not a knock on solar — just a reason to check fit first.
