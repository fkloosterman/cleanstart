## Fix the draft before you replace the window

Old windows leak air and radiate cold, making rooms uncomfortable and driving up heating and cooling. But replacing them is expensive and, judged on energy savings alone, usually takes decades to pay back — so it's best justified by comfort, noise, or a window that's failing.

The high-value moves are cheaper: weatherstrip and caulk the gaps, add interior or exterior storm windows, apply low-e film, and use insulating cellular shades or heavy curtains in winter. Several of these are removable, so renters can use them and take them along.
