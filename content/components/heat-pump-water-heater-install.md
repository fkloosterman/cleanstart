## A big efficiency jump on hot water

A heat-pump water heater pulls warmth from the surrounding air to heat water, using far less electricity than a conventional element. It needs a bit of space and moderate ambient warmth — a basement or utility room is ideal — and makes a light hum.

Federal and local incentives often apply, shortening the payback. Time the swap before your current tank fails so you can choose rather than scramble.
