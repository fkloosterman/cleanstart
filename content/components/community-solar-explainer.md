## Solar you subscribe to

With community solar, a shared array is built somewhere in your region, and subscribers get credits on their utility bills for their portion of what it generates. You don't own or install anything, and many programs require no long-term commitment.

It opens solar savings to renters, condo dwellers, and homes with shaded or unsuitable roofs. Availability and terms vary by state and utility, so it's worth checking what programs serve your address.
