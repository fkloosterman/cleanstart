## Backup that works every day

A whole-home or partial-home battery keeps chosen circuits running when the grid goes down and, on time-of-use rates, can quietly save money by discharging during pricey peak hours. Paired with solar, it stores your own daytime production for the evening.

Batteries added with solar can qualify for the federal residential clean energy credit. Size the backup to the circuits that matter most to you.
