## A cleaner, faster kitchen

A full induction range removes gas combustion from your kitchen air, boils water remarkably fast, and wipes clean because the surface itself stays relatively cool. Most installs need a 240-volt outlet, so budget for an electrician if you're converting from gas.

Check that your cookware is magnetic — a fridge magnet that sticks to the pan's base means it will work.
