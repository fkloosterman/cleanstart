## Small change, quick payback

An LED bulb uses roughly a fifth of the electricity of an old incandescent for the same light and lasts many times longer. Start with the fixtures that run the most hours — kitchen, living room, porch — where the savings land first.

Because bulbs move with you, this is a rare efficiency win that renters keep. Look for ENERGY STAR certified bulbs for reliable quality and color.
