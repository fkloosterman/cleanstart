## Claim part of the cost back

The 25C credit covers a share of qualifying efficiency upgrades — heat pumps, heat-pump water heaters, insulation, air sealing, and efficient equipment — installed in an owner-occupied home, with annual limits by category.

Keep manufacturer certification statements and receipts, and confirm the specific model qualifies before you buy. The credit reduces the tax you owe for the year the work is completed.
