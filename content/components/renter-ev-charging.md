## Where renters plug in

Many apartment EV owners rarely use a home charger: they top up at work, at nearby public fast chargers, or on a regular outlet if a parking spot allows it. Mapping the chargers along your routine tells you quickly whether an EV fits.

If your building has parking, ask about adding a standard or Level 2 outlet — some states and utilities have programs that help multifamily properties install charging.
