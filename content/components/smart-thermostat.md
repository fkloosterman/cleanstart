## Heat and cool only when it helps

A programmable or smart thermostat automatically eases back heating and cooling when you're asleep or away and returns to comfort before you notice. Because conditioning air is the biggest energy cost in most homes, even a few degrees for a few hours a day adds up.

Most models install in under an hour on common systems, and many utilities offer rebates or bill credits for enrolling one in a demand-response program.
