## Stop paying to heat the outdoors

Most homes leak conditioned air through gaps around windows, doors, attic hatches, and where pipes and wires enter. Sealing those leaks and adding insulation keeps warmth in during winter and out during summer, evening out the cold and hot spots that make rooms uncomfortable.

It also shrinks the heating and cooling load, so a heat pump or new system can be smaller and cheaper. That's why weatherization is almost always the first step a good assessment recommends.
