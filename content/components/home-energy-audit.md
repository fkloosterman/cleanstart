## Find the waste before you spend

A professional energy assessment (sometimes a free or low-cost utility program) uses tools like a blower-door test and infrared camera to pinpoint air leaks, missing insulation, and inefficient equipment. You leave with a prioritized list of fixes and their expected savings.

Even a careful DIY walk-through — checking for drafts, inspecting attic insulation depth, and reading your bills across seasons — points you at the biggest opportunities before you commit money.
