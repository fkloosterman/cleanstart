## Maryland's efficiency incentives

Through the EmPOWER Maryland program, the state's utilities provide rebates and incentives for energy-saving upgrades — heat pumps, heat-pump water heaters, insulation, air sealing, and smart thermostats among them — plus free or discounted home energy assessments.

Programs and rebate amounts vary by utility, so check what your provider offers for the specific upgrade you're planning.
