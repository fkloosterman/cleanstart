## Sunlight to electricity

Solar panels contain cells that convert sunlight directly into direct-current electricity; an inverter changes it to the alternating current your home uses. Your home draws from the panels first, and any surplus flows to the grid — often earning a credit on your bill through net metering.

How much a system produces depends on roof orientation, shading, and local sun. Solar pairs naturally with efficiency: the less energy your home wastes, the smaller and cheaper the system you need.
