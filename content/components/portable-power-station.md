## Simple resilience, no wiring

A portable power station is a large rechargeable battery with normal outlets. You charge it from the wall (or a solar panel) and plug essentials into it when the power goes out — no electrician, no permanent install, and it moves with you.

Size it by what you need to run and for how long: a modest unit covers phones, a router, and a CPAP overnight, while larger ones can keep a fridge going through a day-long outage.
