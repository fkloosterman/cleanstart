## Start with the cheap wins

Heating and cooling are the biggest slice of most home energy bills, followed by water heating and everything that plugs in. Before spending on equipment, it pays to see where your energy actually goes and capture the free or low-cost savings first.

A short list that works for almost any home: adjust your thermostat schedule, seal obvious drafts, wash in cold water, and switch remaining bulbs to LED. These cost little, save immediately, and make any later upgrade smaller.
