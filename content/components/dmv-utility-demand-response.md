## Get paid to shift your usage

Utilities serving the DC, Maryland, and Virginia area run programs that reward customers for easing electricity use during peak-demand events — often by briefly adjusting an enrolled smart thermostat — and offer time-of-use rates that reward off-peak usage.

These add up to real bill savings with no purchase, and by trimming peak demand they help keep the regional grid stable. Enrollment details vary by utility.
