## Solar without owning the roof

The most direct path for renters is community solar: subscribe to a share of a local array and see credits on your utility bill, with no installation. Many utilities also offer green-power options that source your electricity from renewables.

If you have a good relationship with your landlord, sharing that solar can raise a property's value and appeal is sometimes worth a conversation — but community solar needs no one's permission but yours.
