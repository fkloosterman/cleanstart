## The basics of going electric

An electric vehicle runs on a battery charged from the grid rather than gasoline. Because electric motors are efficient and electricity is cheaper than gas per mile, most drivers spend far less to fuel and maintain one.

The two questions that decide fit are charging and range: most owners charge at home overnight and rarely think about it, while those without a driveway lean on workplace or public charging. Match a model's range to your longest regular trips, not your rare ones.
