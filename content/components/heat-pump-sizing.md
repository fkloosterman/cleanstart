## Ask for a Manual J load calculation

A right-sized heat pump starts with a **Manual J** load calculation — an industry-standard estimate of how much heating and cooling your specific home needs. Ask any installer to base their proposal on one rather than a rule-of-thumb from square footage.

An oversized system short-cycles: it costs more up front, runs less efficiently, and leaves rooms unevenly conditioned. A good load calc is the difference between comfort and an expensive disappointment.
