## Lowering the price of going electric

Federal clean vehicle credits can reduce what you pay for a qualifying new or used EV, and many buyers can apply the credit at the dealer at the point of sale rather than waiting for tax time.

Which vehicles qualify — and the income limits that apply — change over time and by model, so confirm current eligibility for the specific car before counting on it.
