## Solar savings for DC residents

Solar for All is a DC program designed to bring the benefits of solar to income-qualified households — through community solar subscriptions or rooftop installations — with the goal of cutting electricity bills substantially and at little upfront cost.

Eligibility and enrollment run through the DC Department of Energy & Environment and partner organizations. It reaches both renters and owners, depending on the pathway.
