## Plan the switch before you have to

Replacing heating and cooling with one heat pump is a smoother project when it's planned rather than forced by a mid-winter failure. Get load-calc-based quotes, confirm the system handles your coldest days (with backup heat if needed), and check that ductwork is in good shape.

Pair the project with the federal 25C credit and any local rebates, which can meaningfully cut the net cost.
