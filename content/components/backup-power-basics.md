## Match backup to the need

Resilience is a ladder. At the bottom, a portable power station keeps phones, a router, and a small appliance running for hours. A larger home battery can carry essential circuits — fridge, some lights, medical devices — through a longer outage, and pairs well with solar.

The right rung depends on what you must keep running and for how long. Deciding that first prevents overspending on capacity you'll rarely use.
