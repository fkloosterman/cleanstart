## Plan for the coldest days

Today's cold-climate heat pumps keep homes comfortable well below freezing, but their efficiency falls as it gets colder, and in the coldest snaps some homes rely on supplemental or backup heat. That's normal — not a flaw — and something a good installer sizes for.

Ask how your proposed system handles design-cold days and whether it keeps existing heat as backup. Right-sizing and a sensible backup plan prevent cold-morning surprises.
