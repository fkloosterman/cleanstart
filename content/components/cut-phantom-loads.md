## Stop paying for 'off'

Many electronics — TVs, game consoles, chargers, cable boxes — keep sipping electricity in standby. Individually tiny, together they can be a noticeable slice of a bill across a whole year.

Put clusters of devices on a power strip you can switch off, use smart plugs on the worst offenders, and unplug things you rarely use. It costs almost nothing and takes an afternoon.
