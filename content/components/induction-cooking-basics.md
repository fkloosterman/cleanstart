## Heat the pan, not the kitchen

An induction burner creates a magnetic field that heats the cookware itself, so the surface stays cooler and the kitchen doesn't fill with combustion byproducts the way a gas flame's do. Cooks like the fast boil and fine control.

You don't have to remodel to try it: a portable single-burner induction unit works on any outlet and travels with a renter. It needs magnetic (steel or cast-iron) cookware to work.
